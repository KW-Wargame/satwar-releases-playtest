5 · Walkthrough

Part 1: Listen

Jovian Command

It reports home after every move in a short radio burst. If it sees a warship coming, it goes quiet for six hours. Listen first, from far away.

1Drop a buoy
Press 4 BUOY

A second listener where you are. Two listeners far apart can pinpoint a radio.

2Start the clock
] faster · Space pause

Run at 60× or more and watch TRK 4417 creep toward ORIEL-36.

3Hear it
F2 then click its newest burst

Each bright dash on the waterfall is a radio burst. Pick TRK 4417's latest.

4Find it
FIND DIRECTION · D

Bearings from your two listeners cross where it is.

5Identify it
IDENTIFY SIGNAL · I

Names its command channel, so you can jam just that. This is the bonus goal. Too weak? Wait for a closer burst.

6 · Walkthrough

Part 2: Close In

Jovian Command

Get alongside. The planner does the orbital maths; you choose when, and how much fuel. Arriving isn't stopping: brake with MATCH.

6Pick ORIEL-36
Click it in the Overview

You'll meet the inspector where it's going.

7Plan the trip
F3 · choose CHEAPEST

Each cell is a real trip: wait this long, fly this long, pay this much fuel. CHEAPEST waits 18 h, flies 3 h and costs 1.9 km/s.

8Burn
BURN AT T+18:00

The burn is scheduled for you. Speed time up and coast.

9Match
Press 3 MATCH on arrival

Brakes you onto ORIEL-36's orbit, right beside it.

FuelCHEAPEST leaves you 6.75 of your 8.66 km/s. Every extra trip spends more, and there is no refuelling.

Part 3: Stay Cold, Cut the Line

TRK 4417 · intercepted

MOVE COMPLETE · TARGET IN VIEW · NO ESCORT SEEN

10Go dark
Press 6 DARK · F7 forecast

Stores your waste heat for about 3.5 h, so SEEN turns COLD. You get one, so use it once you are alongside.

11Look closer
Select TRK 4417 · 2

Up close the telescope sees its hull, so you can aim. V visible light, Esc closes.

12Jam
Press 7 JAM

Drowns out its handler's orders. If the key is grey, hover over it: the card ticks off what's missing.

13Hold, then stop
Keep jamming 3 h · 7 again to stop

Cut off for three hours with you within 25 km of it, it backs away and leaves. Alone, it just waits. Jamming adds 2 escalation an hour, so switch it off once it's gone.

JAM is grey?Not a known enemy: identify its signal first (step 5). Can't aim: get closer or let the telescope see it. Out of reach: close in.

8 · After the operation

Endings

The debrief says which goal decided it and what to try next time.

The inspector departsIt was cut off for three hours with you alongside, backed away and left. No debris, no war, ORIEL-36 still working.
It finished its lookAll seven moves done. Try: get alongside sooner and jam earlier.
It went silentIt saw you coming and stopped talking. Try: listen before you move.
Open warEscalation reached 100. Try: fewer loud acts; stop jamming once it's gone.
DebrisSomething was destroyed in the busiest orbit there is. Try: never use the KV here.

10 · Words you'll see

Glossary

TERMS, in the VIEW menu on the header bar, switches the game between plain words and military ones.

ΔvHow much you can still change your speed: your fuel, in km/s.
TrackWhat you believe about a contact. Its ring shows how unsure you are.
RendezvousReaching another craft. Matching its orbit so you stay is the second half.
SIGINTSignals intelligence: listening to other people's radio.
DFDirection finding. Bearings from two places cross at the transmitter.
Command linkThe radio channel a handler uses to send a craft its orders.
Spot / barrageAll jamming power on one known channel, or spread across a band (much weaker).
J/SJam-to-signal: how much louder you are than its handler, at its receiver.
EMCONEmission control. QUIET means you only listen.
EscalationHow close the crisis is to open war. 100 is war.
Going darkStoring waste heat instead of radiating it, so infrared loses you.
RPORendezvous and proximity operations: one craft manoeuvring close to another.
