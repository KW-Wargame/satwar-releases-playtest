PrologueThe Briefing
Jovian Command Det JOVE

Operator. You have the watch on HYDRA BRAVO, our only escort in low Jupiter orbit.

An unflagged inspector is creeping up on ORIEL-36, one of ours. It hasn't fired a shot. It won't.

Make it leave. No debris. No war. Listen first, stay cold, act quietly.

When the JAM key is grey

Hover over it. The card ticks off what it needs:

  • Not a known enemy: identify its signal first (step 5).
  • Can't aim: your track is too loose. Get closer, or let the telescope see it.
  • Out of reach: close in. Before you identify it you can only barrage-jam, which needs you much closer.

Endings

One good ending, four bad ones. The debrief says which goal failed and what to try next time.

GOOD ENDThe inspector departs

It was cut off for three hours, backed away and left. No debris, below war, ORIEL-36 working.

BAD ENDIt finished its look

All seven moves done.

Try: get alongside sooner, jam earlier
BAD ENDIt went silent

It saw you coming and stopped talking.

Try: listen before you move
BAD ENDOpen war

Escalation reached 100.

Try: fewer loud acts, stop jamming once it's gone
BAD ENDDebris

Something was destroyed in the busiest orbit there is.

Try: never use the KV here

10

Words You'll See

TERMS on the rail switches the console between plain words and military ones. Same game either way.

ΔvDelta-v: how much you can change your speed. Your fuel, in km/s.
TrackWhat you believe about a contact. Its ring is how unsure you are.
RendezvousReaching another craft. Matching its orbit so you stay is the second half.
SIGINTSignals intelligence: listening to other people's radio.
DFDirection finding. Two bearings from different places cross at the transmitter.
Command linkThe radio channel a handler uses to send a craft its orders.
Spot / barrageAll power on one known channel, or spread across a whole band (much weaker).
J/SJam-to-signal: how much louder you are than its handler, at its receiver.
EMCONEmission control. QUIET means you only listen.
EscalationHow close the crisis is to open war. 100 is war.
Going darkStoring waste heat instead of radiating it, so infrared loses you.
RPORendezvous and proximity operations: one craft manoeuvring close to another.
