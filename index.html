What is xGEO

02

Features

Computed, not scripted. Filmed in the game.

01

Real orbits

Every transfer is solved, not animated: Lambert's problem under Jupiter's flattened gravity and its four large moons. Lower is faster, and fuel trades against time.

Lambert transfers · J2 from Juno · JPL ephemerides

02

Heat and infrared

Platforms glow by their temperature, and sensors see by computed signal-to-noise against Jupiter's glare. Going dark, or hiding in its shadow, really works.

Planck's law in band · Real sunlight and shadow

03

Signals intelligence

Hear radio traffic across the band, fix it by direction finding, and identify each emitter by what its signal measures.

Friis link budgets · Direction finding · Modulation

04

Electronic warfare

Jam, spoof or listen. Jam-to-signal comes from real antenna patterns and range, so a barrage can fall short where spot jamming works.

ITU antenna patterns · J/S from geometry

05

Forecasts you can plan by

Before you move, see when you will be seen: every sensor's look at you over the coming orbit, in sunlight and in shadow.

Every sensor, every look, computed ahead

06

Platforms and equipment, from specs

Each platform is built from its equipment: dish sizes, power, thrust. Performance is computed from them, and every value cites its source or says it is a design value.

Specs in, performance out · Every number sourced
