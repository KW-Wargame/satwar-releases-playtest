The operation

Something is parked beside our satellite. Nobody asked it to.

TRK 4417 closed on ORIEL-36 in low Jupiter orbit and stopped a few kilometres away. A handler far off radios its every move. You command one warship and a listening station. Get close, stay unseen, find the link and cut it.

How you win

Three moves. No shots fired.

01

Fly by orbit, not by stick

To catch something ahead of you, drop lower: lower is faster. The planner solves every intercept against Jupiter's real, flattened gravity, and MATCH parks you alongside.

Lambert intercepts · Juno-measured gravity

02

Stay cold. Stay unseen.

The inspector carries an infrared camera. If it sees a warship coming, its handler tells it to go quiet. Dump your reactor's heat into the sink, hide in Jupiter's shadow, and keep it talking.

Planck's law in band · Real sunlight and shadow

03

Cut the link

Hear its report, identify the signal, and spot-jam its command uplink. Cut off for three hours, it backs away on its own. Reversible, deniable, and nothing breaks.

Friis link budgets · ITU antenna patterns
