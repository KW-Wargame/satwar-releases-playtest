01

The operation

Real orbits, and the real physics of seeing and being seen. No starfighters and no sound in space: only orbits, heat and patience.

You

HYDRA BRAVO

Your warship: drive, telescope, radio ears, radar and a jammer.

Protect

ORIEL-36

An allied radar satellite. You can't command it, and nothing may damage it.

The inspector

TRK 4417

Closing from behind. A distant handler radios its every move, and that link is its weak point.

Your ears

Ganymede Station

A big dish on Ganymede. It hears radio from millions of kilometres away, but can't move.

1

Close

HYDRA starts higher and behind. To catch something ahead of you, drop lower: lower is faster. The planner finds the transfer, and MATCH stops you alongside.

2

Stay cold

The inspector carries an infrared camera. If it sees a warship coming, its handler tells it to go quiet. Go dark and send your reactor's heat into the sink, so it keeps talking.

3

Cut the link

Hear its report, identify it and spot-jam its command uplink. Cut off for three hours, it backs away. A kill would end it too, and leave debris in the busiest orbit there is.

Based on

The steps of a real uncooperative approach reported in October 2026: stand off, close to a few kilometres, loiter within half a kilometre, then leave. The game shortens weeks to days.

How to play

The first operation in four moves. The handbook has the whole plan, every control and a reference card.

  1. Press New campaign. The tutorial opens, and time is paused until you're ready.
  2. Click ORIEL-36, then TRK 4417, and set time running with ].
  3. Open the planner (F3) on ORIEL-36, take a pick and burn. When you arrive, press MATCH, then DARK.
  4. Open the SIGINT station (F2), click TRK 4417's report and identify it. Then press JAM and hold it until it leaves.

07

The science

The physics is real and every number cites its source. Where the game simplifies or invents, it says so.

Kepler'S Third Law

The moons move by T = 2π√(a³/GM) with NASA's and JPL's values. The game's periods match NASA's observed ones to better than 0.1 %, and the tests check it.

NASA NSSDCA Jupiter and Jovian Satellite Fact Sheets; JPL SSD satellite elements (JUP365, Jacobson 2021)

Low Orbit and J2

ORIEL-36 circles at 1.3 Jupiter radii, once every 4.4 hours. Jupiter is flattened, and so close in its pull bends a three-hour coast by thousands of kilometres: every burn is corrected through the full gravity. Down there is deep in the radiation belts; how hardened each craft is, is a design value.

Folkner et al. (2017), GRL 44, Juno gravity field; Vallado (2013), Fundamentals of Astrodynamics and Applications

Lambert'S Problem

Every intercept is the orbit that joins here-and-now to there-and-then. The solver is the universal-variable method, and reproduces the textbook's worked example.

Curtis (2020), Orbital Mechanics for Engineering Students, 4th ed., Alg. 5.2; Bate, Mueller & White (1971)

Delta-v and Nuclear Thermal

Fuel is delta-v, set by the rocket equation. The 2040-era drives heat hydrogen in a reactor to about 900 s of specific impulse, twice the best chemical rockets, as NASA's Rover/NERVA reactors showed.

NASA Glenn, Ideal Rocket Equation; Borowski, McCurdy & Packard (2012), NASA NTRS 20120003776

Why Nothing Hides

Every warm hull glows, and a telescope sees the share of that glow in its band, by Planck's law. A reactor's waste heat must leave through radiators; laid flat, they show only their edge. Stealth is cold, shiny and edge-on; a burn is megawatts anyone can see.

Mainzer et al. (2023), NEO Surveyor, PSJ 4:224; Widger & Woodall (1976), BAMS 57; Mason (2003), NASA/TM-2003-212596

Bearings, Radar and Listening

One passive sensor gives a line; two give a fix, kept by a Kalman filter. Radar's echo fades as 1/R⁴ but a listener only needs 1/R², so it hears you first.

Bar-Shalom, Li & Kirubarajan (2001); Skolnik (2001), Introduction to Radar Systems, 3rd ed.
