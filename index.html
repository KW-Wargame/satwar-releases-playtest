What you do

FIND

TRK 4417 is a 140 K hull with a low-emissivity skin. Your ship sensors see it from 170,000 km at best. Spread them out, cross your lines of bearing and keep the red ellipse small.

FIX

Every burn lights you up across the system, and radar tells the hostile where you are. Choose when to break emission control: a hard lock now, or stay quiet and close in.

FINISH

Plan intercepts with real orbits and a real fuel budget. Kill the hostile for a DECISIVE result, or guard the freighter so it breaks off: DETERRED.

Who might enjoy it

Anyone curious how space operations would really work: students, teachers, analysts, wargamers, and people in space or electronic warfare education who want to explore the ideas. There are no starfighters and no sound in space here, only orbits, heat and patience. It's an independent hobby project, not an official or endorsed course.

What it is built on

Jupiter's and the Galilean moons' masses and orbits from NASA and JPL; the orbital mechanics of Curtis and of Bate, Mueller and White; nuclear thermal propulsion as NASA tested it; infrared and radar physics from Hudson and Skolnik; Kalman tracking from Bar-Shalom. The full list is below and in the game.

The science

The physics is real and every number cites its source. Where the game simplifies or invents, it says so.

KEPLER'S THIRD LAW

The moons move by T = 2π√(a³/GM) with NASA's and JPL's values. The game's periods match NASA's observed ones to better than 0.1 %, and the tests check it.

NASA NSSDCA Jupiter and Jovian Satellite Fact Sheets; JPL SSD satellite elements (JUP365, Jacobson 2021)
RESONANT ORBITS

The freighter parks at 1.699 × 10⁶ km: exactly twice Ganymede's period. Io, Europa and Ganymede share a 1:2:4 resonance that heats Io into the most volcanic world in the solar system.

Peale, Cassen & Reynolds (1979), Science 203
LAMBERT'S PROBLEM

Every intercept is the orbit that joins here-and-now to there-and-then. The solver is the universal-variable method, and reproduces the textbook's worked example.

Curtis (2020), Orbital Mechanics for Engineering Students, 4th ed., Alg. 5.2; Bate, Mueller & White (1971)
DELTA-V AND NUCLEAR THERMAL

Fuel is delta-v, set by the rocket equation. The 2040-era drives heat hydrogen in a reactor to about 900 s of specific impulse, twice the best chemical rockets, as NASA's Rover/NERVA reactors showed.

NASA Glenn, Ideal Rocket Equation; Borowski, McCurdy & Packard (2012), NASA NTRS 20120003776
WHY NOTHING HIDES

Every warm hull glows: P = εσT⁴A. That light falls off with the square of distance. Stealth means cold, dull and small; a burn means hundreds of megawatts anyone can see.

CODATA 2018 (Stefan–Boltzmann constant); Hudson (1969), Infrared System Engineering
BEARINGS, RADAR AND LISTENING

One passive sensor gives a line; two give a fix, kept by a Kalman filter. Radar's echo fades as 1/R⁴ but a listener only needs 1/R², so it hears you first.

Bar-Shalom, Li & Kirubarajan (2001); Skolnik (2001), Introduction to Radar Systems, 3rd ed.

How to play

  1. Read the operation order, then Begin operation. The clock starts at 3,000× time warp.
  2. HYDRA is selected. In the Maneuver planner, the target is TRK 4417 and the time of flight is 20 hours: check the delta-v and press Execute burn.
  3. Watch the red ellipse. When a flash shows the hostile dodging, re-plan: a shorter time of flight closes faster but costs more.
  4. Inside 120,000 km, think about Radar on. When Engage shows a kill probability, fire.
